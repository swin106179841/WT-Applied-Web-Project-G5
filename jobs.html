Park Ranger

Reference Number: PRK001

Short Description: Join our frontline conservation efforts by managing, protecting, and restoring local national park ecosystems and guiding community engagement.

Salary: $75,000 - $85,000 + Superannuation

Reporting Line: Reports to the Regional Conservation Director

Key Responsibilities

  • Monitor native flora and fauna populations.
  • Conduct guided educational tours for the public.
  • Enforce park regulations, maintain trails, and manage invasive species.

Essential Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Environmental Science, Ecology, or related field.
  • Valid First Aid and CPR certification.
  • Current driver's license with 4WD experience.

Preferable Requirements

  • 2+ years of field experience in land management.
  • Experience in controlled burn operations.

Marine Biologist

Reference Number: MAR002

Short Description: Spearhead our coastal reef restoration projects, monitor marine biodiversity, and collect crucial water quality data for our research initiatives.

Salary: $80,000 - $95,000 + Superannuation

Reporting Line: Reports to the Head of Marine Conservation

Key Responsibilities

  • Collect and analyze water quality and marine life data.
  • Design and implement coral rehabilitation programs.
  • Publish findings in organizational reports and community newsletters.

Essential Requirements

  1. Master's degree in Marine Biology or Oceanography.
  2. Advanced SCUBA diving certification (PADI Divemaster or equivalent).
  3. Proficiency in statistical analysis software (e.g., R, Python).

Preferable Requirements

  • Experience with underwater photography and videography.
  • Commercial boat license.

Wildlife Rehabilitator

Reference Number: WLD003

Short Description: Care for injured, sick, and orphaned native wildlife at our primary sanctuary, with the ultimate goal of safe release back into their natural habitats.

Salary: $65,000 - $72,000 + Superannuation

Reporting Line: Reports to the Sanctuary Manager

Key Responsibilities

  • Provide daily medical care, feeding, and rehabilitation for injured animals.
  • Maintain detailed records of animal intakes, treatments, and releases.
  • Assist in the training of new wildlife volunteers.

Essential Requirements

  • Certificate III in Captive Animals, Veterinary Nursing, or equivalent.
  • Current rabies and tetanus vaccinations.
  • Ability to work flexible hours, including weekends and public holidays.

Preferable Requirements

  1. Previous experience specifically with marsupial rehabilitation.
  2. Strong public speaking skills for leading community workshops.
